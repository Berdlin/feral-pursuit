How to play

You are a lone survivor hunting for supplies while avoiding the feral wolf packs. Each "Day" you collect resources to strengthen your companions and prepare for the night hunt. Survive as many days as you can.

Movement
  • W / A / S / D or arrow keys — move
  • On mobile — use the on-screen joystick
Actions
  • Space — Attack
  • E — Loot chests
  • Q — Inventory
  • M — Toggle minimap

Tip: The blood moon arrives after the collection phase — when it does, run and survive the wolf hunt. Keep companions alive, and use chests cautiously — some are traps.
